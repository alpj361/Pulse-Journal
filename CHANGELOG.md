# Changelog — Vizta App

---

## Version 0.002 — App Store Release Notes

**Release Date:** April 2026

### What's New

- **Chat with Vizta**
  Ask Vizta anything directly from the app. Get answers about news, your Codex, and what's trending — all in a natural conversation. Your chat history is saved and synced across your devices.

- **Fix Search Option on the Codex**
  We fixed an issue where searching for more information about a person or organization in the Codex was not working properly. It now loads results correctly every time.

- **Add Bookmark of Posts on the App**
  You can now bookmark Instagram posts directly inside the Codex. Just paste a link and it will be saved to your personal library so you can come back to it anytime.

- **Correct Splash Screen**
  The app now displays correctly in Spanish from the moment it launches, including system messages and date formats.

- **Correct Layout Issues**
  Fixed a display issue in Settings where the app appeared connected even after logging out. The status now always reflects the real state of your account.

- **Avatars for Codex Actors and Your Profile**
  You can now add a custom avatar to each actor in your Codex, and set your own personal avatar in Settings. Avatars are generated and customizable, making it easier to identify people in your knowledge base at a glance.
